Second-hand buying in Africa runs on luck.

Across Kenya and many African countries, people who buy and sell used electronics and machinery have no reliable place to do it. Facebook Marketplace, WhatsApp groups and classified sites offer little or no verification.

Buyers lose money

Fake listings, stolen goods and poor-quality products leave buyers out of pocket.

Honest sellers go unseen

Genuine sellers struggle to reach customers who trust them.

Usable items become waste

Working electronics and machinery get discarded, adding to the growing e-waste problem. The Global E-waste Monitor reports millions of tonnes generated every year.

From upload to delivery, with a check in the middle.

Trust is built into the order of things: nothing is listed until it has been reviewed.

  1. Sellers upload

    Product details, photographs and proof of ownership.

  2. TechLink reviews

    Every listing is reviewed before publication to reduce fraud.

  3. Buyers browse and talk

    Buyers look through verified products and communicate with sellers.

  4. Trusted delivery partners

    Items move through delivery partners for a safer transaction.
